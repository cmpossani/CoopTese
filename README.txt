METHOD AND SCOPE
Four windows (1995-2002, 2003-2010, 2011-2016, 2017-2022); the archived
surplus-to-assets proxy; the inclusive band [-0.10, 0.20]; complete-window
institutions after the band; geographic minima 3/5; categorical year effects;
institution and location random intercepts; REML. Alternatives are labelled
as sensitivity analyses. The indicator and fixed headquarters geography have
the interpretation limits stated in the article and supplement.

The selected inputs retain institutional CNPJ roots and geographic codes for
audit, excluding personal contact fields. No new reuse license is inferred
for third-party archived inputs. The article and response letter remain
separate submission documents. Appendix A Data and code transparency is at
the end of the revised manuscript, after References.
